How scores and themes are calculated

NPS = (promoters − detractors) ÷ scored responses × 100. Promoters score 9–10, passives 7–8 and detractors 0–6. Passives remain in the denominator; {{ summary.unscored }} unscored interviews in this selection are excluded. Portfolio NPS uses all scored responses, weighted by response count. NPS methodology ↗

Sentiment is analyzed independently from resident comments using local English-language rules. Theme sentences and matching keywords remain linked to their transcripts. Sentiment may miss nuance or sarcasm; review the original wording before acting. No interviews are sent to a model for this analysis.
